Yes. But I'm going to give you a **focused roadmap**, not a "learn everything" roadmap.

# 11. Leadership (Very Important)

Many 8-year interviews focus here.

Prepare stories on:

* Biggest production issue
* Difficult bug
* Architecture decision
* Conflict with teammate
* Mentoring juniors
* Performance optimization
* Delivery under pressure
* Failure and what you learned
* Ownership
* Cross-team collaboration

Use the **STAR** (Situation, Task, Action, Result) format.

---

# 12. Your Own Projects

Be ready to explain in depth:

* Why you chose the architecture.
* Performance optimizations.
* Trade-offs.
* Security considerations.
* How you would scale it.
